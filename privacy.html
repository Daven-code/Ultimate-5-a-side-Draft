Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ultimate 5-a-side Draft handles information when you use this fan-made football draft game, including solo challenges, online rooms, leaderboards and social links.

Last updated: 05 August 2026
Ultimate 5-a-side Draft is an independent fan-made football draft game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, licensed by, or otherwise associated with any football club, league, competition, governing body, player association, game publisher, ratings provider, or data provider. Player ratings shown in the game are for gameplay, comparison and entertainment purposes only.

1. Who operates this website

Ultimate 5-a-side Draft is operated as a fan-made online game. If you have any privacy questions, requests or concerns, you can contact the website at ultimate5asidedraft@gmail.com.

2. Information you may provide

The website is designed to collect as little personal information as practical. Depending on how the game is used, the following information may be provided by users:

  • Display names or usernames entered for local games, online rooms or leaderboard submissions.
  • Room codes and participant names used to run online games with friends.
  • Leaderboard entries, including submitted username, score, game mode, selected challenge information and submission time.
  • Messages you send through the contact form or directly by email when contacting the website.

3. Information collected automatically

Some technical information may be processed automatically to keep the website secure, usable and reliable. This may include device/browser information, approximate location derived from network information, pages visited, referral source, performance information, and basic usage analytics.

The website may use hosting, security, analytics or database tools such as Cloudflare, Firebase and similar service providers. These services may process technical data needed to deliver the website, protect it from abuse, store leaderboard entries, support online rooms and understand general usage.

4. Cookies and local storage

The website may use browser storage such as localStorage to remember temporary game-related information, for example incomplete online bid entries, room state helpers or interface preferences. This information is intended to support gameplay and is stored in the user’s browser unless submitted to the online database as part of an online game or leaderboard entry.

Third-party services used for hosting, analytics, security, advertising or embedded social links may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Run core gameplay features, including online rooms, bidding, drafting and leaderboards.
  • Show scores, rankings and game-mode metadata.
  • Maintain website security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Understand general usage and improve the game experience.
  • Respond to contact emails and support requests.

6. Leaderboards and online rooms

If a score is submitted to a leaderboard, the submitted username, score, game mode and related gameplay metadata may be visible to other visitors. Users should avoid entering real names, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal details as usernames.

Online rooms are intended for temporary gameplay with friends. Room data may include participant display names, game state, selected players, bids, scores and timestamps so that the game can synchronise across devices.

7. Advertising and affiliate links

The website may introduce advertising, sponsorship placements or affiliate links in the future. If adverts are added, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad performance, prevent fraud and serve relevant ads. This Privacy Policy should be reviewed again once advertising is implemented.

8. Sharing information

Information is not sold by the website operator. Information may be processed by service providers that help host, secure, analyse or operate the website. Information may also be disclosed if required by law, to protect the website, or to investigate misuse or security issues.

9. Data retention

Leaderboard entries may be retained so rankings remain available over time. Online room data may be retained for operational, troubleshooting or abuse-prevention purposes, but it is not intended to be a permanent personal record. Contact form messages and email correspondence may be retained as needed to respond to queries and keep a record of support or legal requests.

10. Children and younger users

The website is a general football-themed game and is not intended to collect personal information from children. Users should not submit personal contact details or sensitive information through usernames, online room names or leaderboard entries.

11. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to submit a leaderboard score, use a nickname instead of a real name, clear browser storage through browser settings, and contact the website to ask about removal or correction of leaderboard entries connected to a username. Depending on location, users may have legal rights to access, correct, delete or restrict certain personal information.

12. Player data, ratings and trademarks

Player names, club names, ratings and related information are used for informational, descriptive and gameplay purposes only. Ratings are created and used solely for this game’s fan entertainment experience, including position-based gameplay mechanics, and should not be treated as official ratings, official statistics or endorsements.

No official logos, crests, player images, likenesses or other official branding are used. All trademarks, trade names, player-related rights, ratings, data and other intellectual property rights remain the property of their respective owners.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website develops, especially if advertising, sponsorships, new analytics tools or new online features are added. The updated date at the top of the page will be changed when material updates are made.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, leaderboard removal requests, corrections or general concerns, use the contact form or email ultimate5asidedraft@gmail.com.
